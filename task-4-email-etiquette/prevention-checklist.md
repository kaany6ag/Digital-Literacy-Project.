# &#x20;   **Stay Safe Online  Checklist for College Students**





* Use strong, unique passwords for all accounts and avoid using the same password everywhere.
* Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on important apps like email, banking, and social media.
* Do not click on suspicious links or download attachments from unknown sources.
* Always verify messages or calls claiming to be from banks, delivery services, or companies.
* Keep your devices updated with the latest software and security patches.
* Avoid using public Wi-Fi for logging into sensitive accounts like banking or email.
* UPI Safety: Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money—PIN is only required for sending money.
* UPI Safety: Do not scan unknown QR codes or trust strangers asking for payments online.
* Log out from shared devices and avoid saving passwords on public computers.
* Be mindful of what you share online to protect your privacy and identity.

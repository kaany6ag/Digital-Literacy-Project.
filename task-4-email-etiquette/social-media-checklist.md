&#x20;                                                       **Responsible Social Media Use (Checklist)**





1\) Do’s

* Think before you post—make sure your content is respectful and appropriate.
* Protect your privacy by adjusting account settings and limiting personal information.
* Use social media for learning, networking, and staying informed.
* Verify information before sharing to avoid spreading fake news.
* Maintain a positive and professional online presence.





2\)Don’ts

* Don’t share sensitive personal details like phone number, address, or passwords.
* Don’t post anything offensive, harmful, or disrespectful to others.
* Don’t believe or spread rumors without checking facts.
* Don’t spend excessive time on social media that affects studies or health.
* Don’t engage in online arguments, trolling, or cyberbullying
